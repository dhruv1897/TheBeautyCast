Wordmark

Primary type lockup

The name is one closed-up word, so colour does the job a space would. The drops back into muted grey, Beauty holds the cream, and Cast takes the accent — which is also the half of the name that carries the business. Inter 900 at −2.5% tracking, cut to outlines so nothing depends on a font being installed.

The light cut swaps to the deep accent — #FF7A45 on cream is only 2.4:1 and goes weak in print

The Signal C

Recommended primary mark

A source dot inside two open arcs. It reads as a broadcast signal and as the letter C at the same time, which is exactly the double meaning in the name — you cast creators for brands, and you broadcast them. The aperture opens right at 90°, so the mark has a direction: it points at whatever follows it.

BC Monogram

Maximum legibility

B and C cut from real Inter 900 letterforms — the same type as the wordmark, so it belongs to the same family rather than looking drawn by someone else. The C laps the B by a tenth of an em with an ink knockout between them: enough to interlock, little enough that the B keeps both bowls and never collapses into an E. This is the mark that survives the deepest into small sizes.

Emblem Seal

Large sizes only

The Signal C stamped into a ring with the name and the promise — brands × creators. This is the one that makes the page read as an operation rather than an account, which matters at the exact moment an influencer clicks through from a cold email. Use it on the closing frame and in the deck; it is not a profile picture.
