Usage rights and privacy

Featuring creator content

We only feature clips from creators who have given permission. Every clip carries the creator's handle on screen and the creator is tagged in the post. We use short excerpts under our own commentary, never a full repost. Any creator can ask us to remove their content at any time and we will do it without argument.

Paid campaign content

Usage rights for paid work are agreed per campaign and written into the brief before filming starts. Organic rights and paid advertising rights are priced separately. Creators are paid the rate agreed up front, in full.

What we store

When you submit a form we store the details you gave us so we can contact you about features and campaigns. We do not sell your data or pass it to anyone outside a campaign you have agreed to. Email {{ site.brand.email }} and we will delete your record.

This page is written to be readable rather than legally exhaustive. Have a lawyer review it before you sign your first significant brand contract.
